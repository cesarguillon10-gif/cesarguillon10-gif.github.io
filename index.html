South African judge Navi Pillay wins the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize
Al Jazeera · Friday, Oct 9

The Norwegian Nobel Committee honored her decades of work prosecuting genocide and war crimes.

Why it matters

Pillay, 86, was the first woman of color on South Africa's High Court and later led the tribunal that prosecuted the 1994 Rwanda genocide before serving as the UN's top human rights official. She also chaired a UN panel that concluded Israel committed genocide in Gaza, a finding Israel rejects, making her win politically pointed as well as symbolic. She said she would dedicate the prize "to all of the survivors of international crimes."

The EU (the 27-country bloc) added 743 individuals and 826 companies, mostly tied to weapons production, to its Russia sanctions list.

Why it matters

This is the largest single expansion of EU sanctions since Russia's full invasion of Ukraine began in 2022, and it also targets 77 lawmakers who ran in elections the EU doesn't recognize in Russian-occupied Ukrainian territory. Those listed face a travel ban, frozen assets, and a ban on receiving funds from EU entities. The move comes as the EU also weighs a change that would let it approve future sanctions without needing every member country to agree.
